OUR STORY & ETHOS

Designed with care.
Built in-house.

Thoughtful design, precise execution.

Artifex Interior Solution is a Bengaluru-based design and fabrication studio. We bridge creative architectural ideas and real-world execution, with our own specialized workshop and one team overseeing each project from site measure to handover.

From aluminium kitchen profiles and illuminated 3D storefront signs to stainless-steel staircases, we supervise every cut, joint and installation with care.

HOW WE THINK & WORK

Good spaces start
with good listening.

A dependable result comes from
clear choices and careful craft.

We work with homeowners, offices and businesses to shape spaces around the way people use them. Each project begins with a conversation about function, materials, budget and timing. Drawings and a clear quotation help align the details before fabrication starts.

01

Design for daily use

We consider circulation, storage, light and maintenance alongside the visual finish, so each space works as well as it looks.

02

Workshop-led making

Our Horamavu workshop brings cutting, welding, pre-assembly and quality checks together under one roof.

03

One point of accountability

The Artifex team coordinates the site visit, drawings, fabrication, installation and handover as one continuous process.

Our work spans modular kitchens and wardrobes, architectural metalwork, illuminated 3D signage, windows and partitions, ceiling finishes, flooring and blinds. For each commission, we aim to make the decisions clear and the finished work feel considered.
